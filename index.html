Research

My work sits at the intersection of information economics and contract theory. I am interested in environments where the principal controls what the agent knows before the agent acts: screening, persuasion, and delegation problems in which information itself is the incentive instrument.

Disclosure under Private Preferences

Job market paperWorking paper

Abstract

Placeholder abstract. A principal chooses a signal structure for an agent whose preferences are his private information, then contracts on the agent's action. I show that the optimal disclosure rule is a threshold structure over preference types, and that information provision and monetary incentives are substitutes in a precise sense — expanding disclosure raises the effective cost of the incentive contract.

with [Coauthor Name], [University]Working paper

Abstract

Placeholder abstract. We study a screening problem in which the agent can acquire information about his own type at a cost before contracting.
